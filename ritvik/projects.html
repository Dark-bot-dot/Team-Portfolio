NLP Resume & Job Matcher

A web app that compares a resume to a job description using TF-IDF vectorisation and cosine similarity. Shows a match score, missing skills, and relevant keywords.

PythonNLPHTML/CSSTF-IDF
Open App ↗
How it works
01
Input

User pastes their resume text and a job description.

02
Vectorise

Both texts are converted to TF-IDF vectors to represent word importance.

03
Score

Cosine similarity gives a match percentage and highlights missing skills.
